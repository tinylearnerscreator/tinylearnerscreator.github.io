Who we are

Tiny Learners is a small family project in Springfield, Ohio. Ellie the Elephant, Stella the Star, Bo the Baby Bot and Pip the Dump Truck teach counting, shapes, space, animals and more in short videos. The books use the same friends.

Paperbacks ship from Amazon. Printable PDFs download right after you buy on Etsy, in US Letter and A4, so you print as many copies as you need for home or class.

Illustrations are created with AI assistance, then edited and assembled by us.
