Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy covers the Tiny Learners website and two desktop applications that the Tiny Learners creator runs on their own computer:

  • Tiny Learners Media uploads the creator's own videos to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account.
  • Local Email Bridge lets the creator use the Claude AI assistant to search, read, draft and send email in their own Gmail accounts.

Who uses the apps

The apps are used only by the Tiny Learners creator, with accounts the creator owns. They are not distributed to the public, and they do not collect personal information from viewers, children or other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Google (YouTube and Google Drive): with the account owner's permission, the app uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists on the owner's YouTube channel, and reads video files the owner selects from their own Google Drive.
  • TikTok: with the account owner's permission, the app reads basic profile info (display name and avatar) and uploads or publishes videos to the owner's TikTok account.
  • Gmail (Local Email Bridge): with the account owner's permission, the app reads messages and attachments in the owner's Gmail accounts, creates drafts, sends new messages and replies, and marks a message as read after the owner replies to it. Every message is shown to the owner in an on-screen confirmation and is sent only if the owner approves it.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens, settings, a history of uploads and a local copy of the owner's email are stored only on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are kept encrypted in Windows Credential Manager. Video files are sent directly from that computer to YouTube or TikTok. We run no servers that receive or store this data, and we do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Use with the Claude AI assistant

When the owner asks Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic, about their email, Local Email Bridge passes the relevant messages to Claude so it can answer, draft or reply. No other Gmail data is shared with Anthropic, and no Gmail data is shared with anyone else. Data shared with Claude is handled under Anthropic's Privacy Policy. Tiny Learners does not use Gmail data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services

Tiny Learners Media's and Local Email Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above to the account owner.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or from the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting an app's local data folder removes its stored history and email copy. Local Email Bridge sign-ins are removed with its forget command or from Windows Credential Manager.
