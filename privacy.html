Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy covers the Tiny Learners website and Tiny Learners Media, a desktop application that the Tiny Learners creator runs on their own computer to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the Tiny Learners creator. It is not distributed to the public, and it does not collect personal information from viewers, children or other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Google (YouTube and Google Drive): with the account owner's permission, the app uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists on the owner's YouTube channel, and reads video files the owner selects from their own Google Drive.
  • TikTok: with the account owner's permission, the app reads basic profile info (display name and avatar) and uploads or publishes videos to the owner's TikTok account.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens, settings and a history of uploads are stored only on the owner's computer. Video files are sent directly from that computer to YouTube or TikTok. We run no servers that receive or store this data, and we do not sell, share or transfer it to anyone else.

Google API Services

Tiny Learners Media's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the upload features described above.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or from the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens and history.
